TECHFEST AETHERIAL RENAISSANCE — DEMO RECORDING GUIDE

Recommended demo length: 35–50 seconds
Recording format: MP4, 1080p, landscape or portrait screen recording is acceptable.

1. Open the deployed GitHub Pages URL in Chrome and press F11 for fullscreen.
2. Hold on the hero for 4–5 seconds so the title, edition, date and celestial artwork are visible.
3. Scroll to “The Festival” and show the Compete / Learn / Experience cards.
4. Scroll to Competitions and click the “AI & software” filter, then click “All challenges” to restore all cards.
5. Scroll through the Workshops list and the festival Experiences grid.
6. Open one FAQ item to demonstrate the accordion.
7. Finish at the final CTA and click “Visit official Techfest” only if you want to show the official destination; you do not need to leave your demo recording running on the external site.

Recording tip:
- Windows: Win + Alt + R (Xbox Game Bar) or use OBS Studio.
- Move the cursor slowly; avoid rapid scrolling.
- Do not record personal tabs, bookmarks, notifications or account details.

The page is a static concept website. All calls to action that require official event registration or detailed rules link to the official Techfest website. No backend or sign-in is needed.
